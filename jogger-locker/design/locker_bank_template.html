UP Oval Locker Bank

A 30-door steel locker bank folded into an L so it fits a 2 × 2 m spot. Two wings of three columns meet at a corner block and leave a 1.38 m square to stand in. It has 10 small doors with phone charging and 20 backpack doors, plus a control column with the touch screen and QR payment, and stands 1.85 m tall.

Two door sizes

Every door is 360 mm wide and 550 mm deep inside, so a 20–30 L laptop backpack fits lying flat. Only the height changes. The backpack door is close to a Japanese small coin locker (340 × 350 × 570 mm), which is sized for daypacks. The large doors of proposal 1 did not fit the smaller spot; friends running together can share a backpack door or take two.

Rent at DiliMall and Gyud Food

Neither place publishes its rates. The figures below come from a 2025 graft complaint over both projects, as reported in the news: DiliMall's basic rent is ₱1,000 per m² a month, and Gyud Food charges ₱1,875 per m², or ₱15,000 a month for a stall. The Ombudsman later dismissed the complaint, and rates may have changed since. Use them as a starting point and get a written quote.

Break-even is rentals per joggable window, using the weather page's numbers: 573 joggable windows a year, ₱40 kept per rental (the ₱43 average door less ₱3 of payment fees and SMS), and ₱6,000 of other monthly costs. With ₱40 small doors, DiliMall's break-even drops to 5.0. Many leases also ask for about 3 months' advance and 3 months' deposit: ₱26,880 at DiliMall, ₱50,400 at Gyud Food and ₱100,800 for a full Gyud stall. Electricity is usually billed on top.

Both places open at 7 AM, and your morning peak starts at 5 AM.

Robinsons Easymart at DiliMall and Gyud Food both run 7 AM to 10 PM. Mornings are 306 of the 573 joggable windows. If nobody can reach the lockers before 7 AM, only about 369 windows stay sellable, and break-even rises by about half (5.4 becomes 8.4 at DiliMall). Ask for a spot on an outside wall, under a roof, that can be reached from 5 AM, with lighting and CCTV.

Gyud Food Hub

  • Where: the UP Diliman Food Hub on E. Jacinto St. near University Avenue, across from the College of Fine Arts. Guides put it 5–10 minutes' walk from the Oblation, and visitors mention stopping there after running the Oval.
  • Hours: 7 AM to 10 PM daily, open-air, with free parking.
  • Rent: the highest per m². Reports say some vendors backed out over high rent.
  • Fit: the right crowd for this idea. It is worth the extra rent only if they will lease a small wall spot by the m² with early access.

DiliMall

  • Where: the old UP Shopping Center site on Laurel Ave., G. Apacible, Pio Valenzuela and Roces St., near the Parish of the Holy Sacrifice and the UP Infirmary. Walk it from the Oval before deciding.
  • Hours: Robinsons Easymart there opens 7 AM to 10 PM. Other tenants vary.
  • Rent: about half of Gyud Food's per m².
  • Fit: cheaper, with chain-store traffic. The mall is controversial with student groups, which may matter for a student-facing brand.

Questions for the leasing offices

  • Can I rent a 2 × 2 m spot, ideally in a corner or against two walls, or only full stalls?
  • Is the rate per m² per month? Does it include VAT and common-area charges?
  • Can people reach the lockers from 5 AM, or 24 hours? Is there a guard or CCTV at night?
  • Is there a covered outside wall with a 220 V outlet? Is electricity sub-metered or a flat fee?
  • Can I bolt the unit to the floor and wall, and put up a sign?
  • How much advance and deposit, what minimum term, and what yearly increase? Would you take a share of sales instead of fixed rent?
  • Who handles disputes over lost items, and do I need insurance?

Notes for the fabricator

  • Steel: 0.8 mm (22 gauge) powder-coated shell and shelves; 1.0 mm (20 gauge) doors with a folded stiffener. Use a light color so the inside stays cooler.
  • Locks: 12 V electronic cabinet locks with a door-closed sensor. They must stay locked if the power fails. A master override lives inside the service cabinet.
  • Power: one 220 V outlet, about 150 W at peak for locks, screen, router, lights and charging. A battery backup rides through brownouts.
  • Air: vent slots at the top and bottom of every door and in the back panel, so sweaty clothes do not leave a smell.
  • Charging: USB-A and USB-C ports in the 10 small doors.
  • Corner: build each wing as its own carcass and bolt both to a 620 × 620 mm corner block. Leave out the side panel between the control column and the corner block, so the service cabinet reaches into the corner for the battery backup, router and spare locks.
  • Hinges: hang every door on the side away from the inner corner: left-hand hinges on the left wing, right-hand hinges on the right wing.
  • Light: an LED strip in the sign band. It is still dark at 5 AM all year round.
  • Security: bolt to the floor and, in a corner, to both walls; point a CCTV camera into the L, and raise an alarm if a door is forced.
  • Outdoor sites: add the 300 mm canopy with a drip edge, seal the electronics box, and keep the unit out of the afternoon sun.
  • Reach: the system should hand out low doors first and use the top row only when the rest are full.
