    What's in the model

    Size
    2.80 m wide, 0.62 m deep, 1.85 m tall, in seven 400 mm columns
    Doors
    6 small, 21 backpack and 3 large, numbered column by column
    Control column
    10-inch touch screen, instruction plate and a locked service cabinet
    Outdoors
    Turn on the canopy for a site without a roof; the lit sign band covers 5 AM
    3D file
    up-oval-locker.glb, openable in 3D viewer apps, with door animations
    Screen and QR
    Sample screens and a sample QR code that cannot be paid

    Built with three.js r147 from the same door layout as the UP Oval Locker Bank drawings. Companion pages: UP Oval Locker System and UP Oval Locker Locks.
