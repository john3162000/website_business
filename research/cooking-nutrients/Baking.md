# Baking

Back to [[00 Index - Cooking and Nutrients]] · compare [[Grilling and Roasting]]

**Baking** is cooking in an oven with dry hot air, usually 160–220 °C, for 15–60 min or more.

- **No leaching**, but **long time** at high heat. Vitamins destroyed by time and heat (thiamin, vitamin C, folate, vitamin D) can drop. Thiamin losses were second only to boiling in Kimura et al. 1990. Long oven baking was the worst method for vitamin D in Jakobsen & Knuthsen 2014.
- Minerals, protein, carbs, fiber: kept.
- **Baking soda / baking powder** makes doughs alkaline, which destroys thiamin.
- Baking does not remove oxalate (Chai & Liebman 2005).
- **Yeast fermentation** in bread (pandesal, ensaymada) lowers phytate in flour. See [[Fermentation]].

Filipino examples: pandesal, ensaymada, bibingka, baked bangus, embutido (often steamed instead), leche flan (steamed or baked), cassava cake.

**In SarapSulit:** `BAKE_VETO` and the `oven` appliance detect it. Most baked recipes are sweets and breads where vitamin C and folate don't matter much.
