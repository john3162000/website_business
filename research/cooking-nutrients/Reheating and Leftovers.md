# Reheating and leftovers

Back to [[00 Index - Cooking and Nutrients]]

Filipino households often cook a big pot (adobo, sinigang, menudo) and reheat it over a day or two ("mas masarap kinabukasan"). Carinderias keep food warm for hours.

## Nutrients
- **Hot-holding** (keeping warm): vitamin C, folate and B6 lose **more than 10% after 2 hours** (Williams 1996 review of 66 studies). Retinol, thiamin, riboflavin and niacin were fairly stable.
- **Chilled 24 h at 3 °C, then reheated**: vitamin C and folate losses can exceed **30%** (Williams 1996).
- Minerals, protein, carbs, fiber, fat: unchanged.
- **Rice cooled overnight** gains a little resistant starch (0.64 → 1.65 g/100 g after 24 h at 4 °C and reheating; Sonia et al. 2015). See [[Carbohydrates, Starch and Fiber]].

## Food safety
- Cooked rice left at room temperature can grow *Bacillus cereus*, whose toxin survives reheating. Refrigerate leftovers within about 2 hours.
- Reheat until steaming hot throughout.
