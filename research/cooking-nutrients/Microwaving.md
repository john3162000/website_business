# Microwaving

Back to [[00 Index - Cooking and Nutrients]] · compare [[Steaming]]

Microwaves heat water inside the food. Cooking is fast and uses little or no added water.

- **Vitamin C:** microwaving generally kept the **most** vitamin C in Lee et al. 2018 (boiling kept the least). Retention ~**70–95%** with little water.
- **Vitamin K:** in the same study microwaving caused the largest K loss in two greens and the smallest in two others. Effects depend on the vegetable.
- **B12:** microwave heating of raw beef, pork and milk destroyed **30–40%** of B12 (Watanabe et al. 1998).
- **Uneven heating** can leave cold spots: a food-safety issue when reheating. See [[Reheating and Leftovers]].

**Myth:** "microwaving destroys nutrients" or "makes food radioactive". **False.** For vegetables it is one of the gentler methods. See [[Myths and facts]].
