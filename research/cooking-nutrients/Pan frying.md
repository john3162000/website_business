# Pan frying (shallow frying)

Back to [[00 Index - Cooking and Nutrients]] · see [[Fat and Frying]], compare [[Deep frying]], [[Sauteing and Stir-frying]]

**Pan frying** uses enough oil to cover the bottom of the pan or part of the food; the food is turned once or twice.

- **Fat uptake** can be **higher** than deep frying for meat, poultry and fish (Bognár 1998), because the food sits longer in oil at a lower average temperature.
- Vitamin and mineral retention: similar to deep frying (short time, little water).
- Filipino examples: daing na bangus, tortang talong, longganisa, tocino (sugar: more browning), fried egg, pritong tilapia in shallow oil.

**In SarapSulit:** `PAN_FRY_STEP` sets `frying: "shallow"` with a 2–5% (plain) to 5–10% (coated) uptake range. Consistent with Bognár.
