# Cooking and nutrients: index

These notes answer one question for **SarapSulit**: *what happens to a food's nutrients when it is cooked?* The goal is that the engine can later turn "raw ingredient" nutrient values (from the FNRI Philippine Food Composition Table) into "what is on the plate" values.

**Status:** research only, written 2026-10-29. Nothing here has been applied to the engine or the site. [[Applying it to SarapSulit]] is a **proposal for the owner to approve**.

## How sure are we?

Each note marks claims as:
- **Well established**: many studies agree, or an official table says so.
- **Moderate**: a few good studies agree, but numbers vary a lot.
- **Weak / to verify**: one small study, a secondary source, or a number I could not check against the primary document from this session (the USDA and Bognár PDFs could not be downloaded here; see [[Retention factor datasets]]).
